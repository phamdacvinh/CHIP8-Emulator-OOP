5 game nen choi, on dinh, de bam phim
Chay lenh trong PowerShell tai thu muc: C:\chip8-final

Phim chung:
- ESC: thoat game
- CHIP-8 key 4: mui ten trai / A / Q
- CHIP-8 key 5: W
- CHIP-8 key 6: mui ten phai / D / E
- CHIP-8 key 1: phim 1
- CHIP-8 key 8: S
- CHIP-8 key 0: X

1. Space Invaders
Lenh:
.\build\chip8.exe 12 4 "roms\Space Invaders [David Winter].ch8"
Cach choi:
- Nhan W de bat dau / ban dan
- Mui ten trai hoac A/Q de di trai
- Mui ten phai hoac D/E de di phai

2. Tetris
Lenh:
.\build\chip8.exe 12 5 "roms\Tetris [Fran Dachille, 1991].ch8"
Cach choi:
- Mui ten trai hoac A/Q: xoay khoi
- W: di chuyen sang trai
- Mui ten phai hoac D/E: di chuyen sang phai
- Phim 1: roi nhanh

.\build\chip8.exe 12 6 "roms\Blitz [David Winter].ch8"

- Ban trung tau ngam de ghi diem
